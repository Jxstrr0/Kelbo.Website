About

I'm Kelbo, and I'm based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. I played university football on a scholarship.

I got into making games through my brother's interest in coding and my own interest in engineering of all kinds. It gives my overactive mind somewhere to go, and I get to build games that play the way I want them to. I make them under the name Longlight Interactive, mostly for fun, for myself and for anyone who enjoys the same style of game.

I'm also in my first year of day trading part-time. I like high-pressure situations, and I enjoy digging into the data and building a thesis before I take a trade. I post my calls and trades in my trading journal, wins and losses both.

Outside of games and trading, I'm building small tools and AI projects. This site is a collection of my active projects and passions. Some of it is mostly done and some of it is still just a plan, so if you check back every once in a while, you should see things move down the field.

When I'm not building or trading, I'm usually watching F1 or football. I used to race stock cars too, which is where Prairie Engine Works comes from. I'm also a big Survivor and Big Brother fan.

Play a game or two, and have a look at my trading theses. If anything here catches your eye, feel free to reach out.
