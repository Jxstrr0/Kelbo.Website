# Art credits

Everything in `assets/art/` and `assets/films/` is AI-generated, made by Kelbo
with Higgsfield (November 2026), cut down for the web with ffmpeg. It is
mood art, not gameplay, and the game pages say so.

- `art/prairie-engine-works.jpg` (+ `@2x`): a crate engine on the dyno at dusk.
- `art/lombard.jpg` (+ `@2x`): the pawn shop counter over a Mostograd canal.
- `films/prairie-engine-works.mp4` (+ `.jpg` still): the engine on the dyno, 10 s.
- `films/scrapline.mp4` (+ `.jpg`): two robots meeting in the arena, 10 s.

The gallery (`gallery.html`) reuses the two key art pictures and the
Scrapline film still above, labelled as AI-generated. Everything in
`assets/gallery/` is the games' own work: scenes captured from the live
games, or renders from their own Blender scripts (textures from other
makers are credited in each game's credits).
