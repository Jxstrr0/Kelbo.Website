Prediction Tracker

Make a call, say how sure you are, and come back once you know how it turned out. Over time, this shows whether your 80% calls actually come true 80% of the time.

Your calls are saved in this browser on this device only. Nothing gets sent anywhere, so nobody else can see them (including me). Use Backup at the bottom to save a copy or move them to another device.

This browser isn't letting the page save, so your calls will be gone when you close the tab. Export a backup before you leave.
